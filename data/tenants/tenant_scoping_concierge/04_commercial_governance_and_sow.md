## 1. Standard Commercial Terms & Milestone Escrow

Every client engagement with Prateeq Studio is executed under an immutable Statement of Work (SOW) brief governing payments, deliverables, intellectual property, and warranties:

1. **50/50 Milestone Escrow Structure:**
   - **50% Upfront Deposit:** Required prior to initiating architecture design, database provisioning, and codebase development.
   - **50% Final Balance:** Due upon staging sign-off and milestone acceptance, strictly prior to production DNS domain mapping and repository handover.
2. **Scope Creep & Change Order Protocol:**
   - Any feature, API integration, or page not explicitly documented in the signed SOW brief is designated as a **Change Order**.
   - Change Orders are estimated independently and billed under a Phase 2 Add-On contract; they never delay the timeline or milestone payout of the primary agreement.
3. **Intellectual Property (IP) Transfer:**
   - 100% of custom source code, database schemas, and digital assets transfer to the client upon receipt of 100% of project contract fees.
4. **Third-Party Infrastructure Direct Billing:**
   - Cloud hosting (Vercel), managed databases (Supabase), domain registrars, and external AI API usage (OpenAI, Resend) are billed directly to client-owned accounts.

## 3. Sales Partner & Middleman Partnership Agreement

Prateeq Studio operates an official referral and sales partnership framework enabling agency brokers, consultants, and freelance sales representatives to earn tiered commissions on closed software contracts.

### Tiered Commission Schedule
Commission is calculated strictly on **Net Funds** (amounts actually cleared and retained in studio bank accounts, net of taxes and refunds):

| Contract Value Tier | Contract Value (INR) | Contract Value (USD) | One-Time Project Commission Rate | Recurring Care Plan Commission Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Band A** | Up to ₹75,000 | Up to $1,000 USD | **10%** | **10% Ongoing** |
| **Band B** | ₹75,001 – ₹249,999 | $1,001 – $3,299 USD | **12%** | **10% Ongoing** |
| **Band C** | ₹250,000 and Above | $3,300 USD and Above | **15%** | **10% Ongoing** |

### Worked Numerical Example (Band C Engagement)
$$\text{Signed Project Contract Value} = ₹320,000 \quad (\text{Band C Rate} = 15\%)$$
$$\text{Total Partner Commission} = ₹320,000 \times 0.15 = ₹48,000$$

- **Milestone 1 Payout:** Upon receipt of Client's 50% Upfront Deposit ($₹160,000$), Partner receives 50% of commission ($₹24,000$) within **48 business hours**.
- **Milestone 2 Payout:** Upon receipt of Client's Final 50% Balance ($₹160,000$), Partner receives the remaining 50% ($₹24,000$) within **48 business hours**.
- **Recurring Care Payout:** If Client enrolls in a ₹10,000/mo Standard Care Plan, Partner earns $₹1,000/\text{month}$ ($10\%$) for the lifetime of the client retainer.

### Core Middleman Protection Rules
1. **Rule 3.1 (No Out-of-Pocket Liability):** Developer will never disburse partner commissions out-of-pocket prior to client funds clearing bank accounts.
2. **Rule 3.2 (Proportional Disbursement):** Commission is disbursed proportionally (50% on deposit, 50% on final balance) within 48 business hours.
3. **Rule 4.1 (Non-Circumvention):** Partner agrees not to bypass Developer or refer introduced qualified prospects to third-party developers without written consent.
4. **Rule 4.2 (Codebase & IP Ownership):** All codebase assets remain property of Developer until 100% of contract fees are paid by Client.
